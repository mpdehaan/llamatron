Hello World: 01_hello_world.yml ​

The smallest set there is: a C major scale up in the first scene, down in the second, and up again in the third while a second track holds a C chord for a quarter note, every note a sixteenth. Three seconds long. Open it to see what a pattern, a track and a scene are, play it first when setting up real-time MIDI (docs/MIDI_SETUP.md), and record it first (docs/RECORDING.md).

Patterns Only: 02_pachelbel.yml ​

The opening of Pachelbel's Canon in D for three violins and a cello, using nothing but patterns and scenes, the way you might copy a score in. Because it's a canon, the violins share the same patterns, each a scene behind the one before.

Key Changes: 03_mary_keychange.yml ​

Mary Had a Little Lamb in four keys, one a scene: C major, C minor, E major, and whole tone on F#. The tune and its bass are one pattern each, written in scale degrees, and only each scene's Scale changes, so it shows how a key change is one field on the Scene page and no pattern moves.

examples/learning/03_mary_keychange.yml · C major · 112 BPM · 1:09 · 4 scenes · 2 tracks

Scales: 04_scale.yml ​

Every scale llamatron knows, each run up an octave and back down from D, one scene per scale, in the order of the scale picker. Use it to preview a scale before choosing one. The demo shows that a scene can set its own scale, and that a degree past the top of a scale wraps up an octave.

examples/learning/04_scale.yml · D major · 120 BPM · 3:01 · 47 scenes · 1 tracks

Voice Leading: 05_voice_leading.yml ​

Block chords on the piano over a bass in D major, three progressions each played three ways, back to back: in root position, every chord jumping up from its root; inverted by hand with the chord editor's Inv. row; and through the Voice leading generator, which plays each chord in the inversion and octave nearest the chord before. The last scene keeps the root in the bass.

Progressions: 06_chord_progression_generator.yml ​

A pop song in C major made from three patterns one bar long, an arpeggio, a bass figure and a chord pad, each written over chord I and played through a Progression, a different one in every scene: Pop, Fifties, Andalusian in A minor, Pachelbel's eight chords, Pop again with the pad's chords inverted so that they move less, and Pop with the pad through a Voice leading generator.

Twelve-Bar Blues: 07_blues_chords.yml ​

A shuffle blues in G, where the walking bass, the comping chords and the riff are each one bar long, played through the twelve-bar blues and the quick-change blues. The comp's chord is a dominant seventh, which stays one on every chord of the blues, and the riff's blue note moves with the chords.

Ties: 08_tie.yml ​

An original downtempo piece in D Mixolydian whose pad draws its chords with the Tie row rather than with gates, so a chord lasts as long as the ties that follow it, and whose ending holds a bass note with ties alone, longer than any gate could make it. The demo also brings in the tracks one at a time, morphs a tune into its answer, grabs an echo and slides a filter, so it's the closest thing here to a finished song.

examples/learning/08_tie.yml · D mixolydian · 84 BPM · 2:59 · 9 scenes · 8 tracks

Grooves: 09_groove.yml ​

A twelve-bar organ shuffle in G minor, llamatron's own tune in the spirit of a sixties organ trio: a Hammond lick, walking pedals, seventh chords bubbling through an arpeggiator, and drums. The song shuffles with the factory groove 8th swing 66, the pedals and pads keep grooves of their own, the drums are off so the kick and snare stay on the grid, and the scenes play it straight, drag it behind the beat with a groove of the set's own, and shuffle it harder, so you can hear how the track's choice wins over the scene's, which wins over the song's.

examples/learning/09_groove.yml · G minor · 130 BPM · 1:51 · 5 scenes · 5 tracks

Articulate: 15_acid_articulate.yml ​

An acid house line played the whole way through, whose notes lengthen from short blips into long, gliding, joined notes over each scene while its filter opens, with no gate changed. The demo sets Articulate on the scenes, on the line itself and as a generator that bounces between short and long every bar, and a register bound to CC 74 opens the filter as the notes lengthen.

Swell: 16_bolero_swell.yml ​

An original piece in three in the manner of Ravel's Bolero: one tune and one snare rhythm, nine scenes of one unbroken crescendo from soft to full force, climbing evenly in loudness, without one velocity changed in a pattern. Each scene's Swell starts where the one before ended, the first three scenes keep the same instruments so that only the loudness climbs, and in the last the timpani run through a Swell generator that accents every downbeat. Use velocity-sensitive instruments to hear it.

Hocketing: 19_hocket_shanty.yml ​

The Wellerman, held by a muted track called SILENT melody, with up to four voices taking turns at its notes, one note each, through a Hocket generator that names the melody's track as its source. That's called hocketing. One voice plays every note, two alternate, and four each play every fourth note; each voice has a sound of its own, flute, clarinet, oboe and violin, to hear the tune pass between them.

examples/learning/19_hocket_shanty.yml · A minor · 100 BPM · 2:14 · 7 scenes · 5 tracks

Few Patterns: 20_arrangement_shanty.yml ​

The Wellerman again, with eight parts built from only four note patterns. The harmony is the melody patterns transposed on its track, the bass grabs the roots of the pad's chords, and a counter-line grabs the melody in its own rhythm. Read this one to see how to set up a song so that changing one pattern changes every part that follows it.

Grabbed Parts: 21_look_ma_no_notes.yml ​

A gospel song in A flat where the bass, the piano and the lead hold no notes at all. The organ holds one seventh chord a bar, moved through the changes by Progression generators, and every other part is Grab steps that take the root of the organ's chord and build on it: the bass an octave down, the piano's chords from its Chord row, and the lead three ways, from the register rows, from an arpeggiator and from climbing and falling ratchets. The last chorus goes up a half step, and every part follows.

examples/learning/21_look_ma_no_notes.yml · Ab major · 76 BPM · 2:57 · 6 scenes · 7 tracks

Registers: 22_registers.yml ​

An acid techno riff of five notes, changed bar by bar by the four registers, AX to DX. AX, BX and CX each move a different note of the riff, in cycles of 4, 6 and 5 bars, then all three at once. Then DX fades the riff, kick and hat out together over six repeats of the last scene.
