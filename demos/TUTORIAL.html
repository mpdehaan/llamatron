Hello World: 01_hello_world.yml ​

The smallest set there is: a C major scale up in the first scene, down in the second, and up again in the third while a second track holds a C chord for a quarter note, every note a sixteenth. Three seconds long. Open it to see what a pattern, a track and a scene are, play it first when setting up real-time MIDI (docs/MIDI_SETUP.md), and record it first (docs/RECORDING.md).

examples/learning/01_hello_world.yml · C major · 120 BPM · 0:03 · 3 scenes · 2 tracks

Key Changes: 03_mary_keychange.yml ​

Mary Had a Little Lamb in four keys, one a scene: C major, C minor, E major, and whole tone on F#. The tune and its bass are one pattern each, written in scale degrees, and only each scene's Scale changes, so it shows how a key change is one field on the Scene page and no pattern moves.

Scales: 04_scale.yml ​

Every scale llamatron knows, each run up an octave and back down from D, one scene per scale, in the order of the scale picker. Use it to preview a scale before choosing one. The demo shows that a scene can set its own scale, and that a degree past the top of a scale wraps up an octave.

examples/learning/04_scale.yml · D major · 120 BPM · 3:01 · 47 scenes · 1 tracks

Voice Leading: 05_voice_leading.yml ​

Block chords on the piano over a bass in D major, three progressions each played three ways, back to back: in root position, every chord jumping up from its root; inverted by hand with the chord editor's Inv. row; and through a Chords generator's Voice leading, which plays each chord in the inversion and octave nearest the chord before. The last scene keeps the root in the bass.

Ties: 08_tie.yml ​

An original downtempo piece in D Mixolydian whose pad draws its chords with the Tie row rather than with gates, so a chord lasts as long as the ties that follow it, and whose ending holds a bass note with ties alone, longer than any gate could make it. The demo also brings in the tracks one at a time, morphs a tune into its answer, grabs an echo and slides a filter, so it's the closest thing here to a finished song.

Grooves: 09_groove.yml ​

A twelve-bar organ shuffle in G minor, llamatron's own tune in the spirit of a sixties organ trio: a tonewheel organ lick, walking pedals, seventh chords bubbling through an arpeggiator, and drums. The song shuffles with the factory groove 8th swing 66, the pedals and pads keep grooves of their own, the drums are off so the kick and snare stay on the grid, and the scenes play it straight, drag it behind the beat with a groove of the set's own, and shuffle it harder, so you can hear how the track's choice wins over the scene's, which wins over the song's.

examples/learning/09_groove.yml · G minor · 130 BPM · 1:51 · 5 scenes · 5 tracks

Swell: 14_bolero_swell.yml ​

An original piece in three in the manner of Ravel's Bolero: one tune and one snare rhythm, nine scenes of one unbroken crescendo from soft to full force, climbing evenly in loudness, without one velocity changed in a pattern. Each scene's Velocity Ramp In starts where the scene before ended, the first three scenes keep the same instruments so that only the loudness climbs, and in the last the timpani run through a Shapes generator whose Swell accents every downbeat. Use velocity-sensitive instruments to hear it.

examples/learning/14_bolero_swell.yml · C major · 72 BPM · 3:00 · 9 scenes · 7 tracks

Hocketing: 17_hocket_shanty.yml ​

The Wellerman, held by a muted track called SILENT melody, with up to four voices taking turns at its notes, one note each, through a Hocket generator that names the melody's track as its source. That's called hocketing. One voice plays every note, two alternate, and four each play every fourth note; each voice has a sound of its own, flute, clarinet, oboe and violin, to hear the tune pass between them.

Few Patterns: 18_arrangement_shanty.yml ​

The Wellerman again, with eight parts built from only four note patterns. The harmony is the melody patterns transposed on its track, the bass grabs the roots of the pad's chords, and a counter-line grabs the melody in its own rhythm. Read this one to see how to set up a song so that changing one pattern changes every part that follows it.

Grabbed Parts: 19_look_ma_no_notes.yml ​

A gospel song in A flat where the bass, the piano and the lead hold no notes at all. The organ holds one seventh chord a bar, moved through the changes by Chords generators, and every other part is Grab steps that take the root of the organ's chord and build on it: the bass an octave down, the piano's chords from its Chord row, and the lead three ways, from a draw's rows, from an arpeggiator and from climbing and falling ratchets. The last chorus goes up a half step, and every part follows.

Guitarify: 21_guitarify.yml ​

A tour of guitar playing in twenty-one short scenes, each a way a guitar is played, on seven General MIDI guitars and nothing else: chords as written strummed in Strum mode, a country bass note and strum, picking with thumb and fingers, bluegrass crosspicking, jazz comping, a blues in open G, power chords, a surf tremolo, the folk strum, funk scratching, a singer's rolled chords, metal gallops and a twelve bar shuffle. Every pattern is only a row of chords or low notes, and the Guitarify generator of each scene does the playing, so the demo shows what its Strum mode and its Shape and Strokes halves do to the same kind of pattern.

examples/learning/21_guitarify.yml · G major · 104 BPM · 3:19 · 21 scenes · 7 tracks

Follow Chords: 22_follow_chords.yml ​

A song in A minor whose chords are written in one place, a muted track called SILENT Chords. The bass, the pad and the arpeggio each play one bar, written over chord I, through a Chords generator in Follow Progression mode that moves their notes to whatever chord that track plays. The chords are heard plainly first, then the band plays without following and sounds stuck on one chord, then the bass follows alone, then everyone. Later scenes add a tune, change the chords under the same patterns, change chord twice a bar, and follow chords that a progression makes on that track.

Musician: 24_musician.yml ​

A band in G major whose players each have one bar. The first scene plays every pattern as written, all the time. The scenes after it hand the same patterns to Musician generators: in the verse the rhythm guitar plays softer, the hi-hat keeps to the beats, the organ sits out and the lead guitar plays only where the tune rests; in the chorus everyone plays and the organ holds each chord; in the breakdown the bass plays one hit a bar and the rhythm guitar is muted.

Accents: 26_accents.yml ​

A funk groove in E minor with no velocity typed on any step. Each part plays at the Default velocity of its track, and a step whose Accent switch is on plays louder by the track's Accent. The first scene plays four bars with every switch off, so the groove sounds flat. The scenes after it switch accents on a part at a time: the hats on the beat, then off the beat, then a snare whose quiet ghost notes sit under an accented backbeat, then the bass and the keys, then hats accented in threes across the beat. The last scene plays the first again, to hear what the switches did.

examples/learning/26_accents.yml · E minor · 100 BPM · 2:19 · 7 scenes · 5 tracks

Pattern Lists: 27_pattern_lists.yml ​

A hard rock riff in E minor made of four patterns of one bar each: the main figure, an answer, a run up and a stop. Every clip of the riff plays a list of those patterns, as Pattern Lists describes, and every section of the song is the same four bars in another order. The first scene plays the four bars once. The verse and the chorus list them two ways, with the bass listing bars of its own and the drums playing three bars of the beat and a fill. The scenes after show Pattern order: alternate, pendulum, and first, then random. The seventh scene sets Step direction, which plays the steps inside each pattern backward while the patterns keep their order.

examples/learning/27_pattern_lists.yml · E minor · 120 BPM · 2:24 · 8 scenes · 5 tracks

Draws: 28_draws.yml ​

Draws in eleven scenes, every note written as the same C in a chromatic scale, so that everything that moves is a draw. A count-in plays C, C#, D and D# from a draw that moves forward. Four scenes deal twelve-tone rows from a serial draw, which plays every pitch of the octave once before any comes again: plainly, passed between two instruments, with rests and leaps, and in pairs. Four scenes over a bass add two draws on alternate steps, add them together, pull one against the other, and add all three. The last two scenes gate notes by draws: a melody that grows as a draw counts the bars, and a bell whose rhythm comes from a draw that its own condition redraws.

examples/learning/28_draws.yml · C chromatic · 108 BPM · 2:58 · 11 scenes · 6 tracks
