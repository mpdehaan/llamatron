Accents: 28_accents.yml ​

A funk groove in E minor with no velocity typed on any step. Each part plays at the Default velocity of its track, and a step whose Accent switch is on plays louder by the track's Accent. The first scene plays four bars with every switch off, so the groove sounds flat. The scenes after it switch accents on a part at a time: the hats on the beat, then off the beat, then a snare whose quiet ghost notes sit under an accented backbeat, then the bass and the keys, then hats accented in threes across the beat. The last scene plays the first again, to hear what the switches did.

Advanced Conditionals: 27_advanced_conditionals.yml ​

Conditional Execution in six scenes. In the first, four tracks each play one note a bar, each at a different place in the bar: one in every bar, one in every second bar, one in every third bar, and one whose pitch climbs with the count of the bars, so that the counting is heard. In the second, one pattern of four chords plays eight times on one guitar, and the chain's four Guitarify generators take turns, so the same chords are strummed, then picked, then chugged, then rolled, twice round. The last three scenes show the statements a step of a pattern can hold: Mute When plays a bass fill only in every fourth bar while two notes take turns bar by bar, Restart When makes a riff start over after half a bar twice before playing whole, and Stop When ends the song after a phrase has played exactly two and a half times. Before that last scene, two registers show their Reset behavior: one reverses, so a single note walks up the scale and back down, and one overflows, so a single note visits the same five pitches in a shuffled order.

Draws: 30_draws.yml ​

Draws in eleven scenes, every note written as the same C in a chromatic scale, so that everything that moves is a draw. A count-in plays C, C#, D and D# from a draw that moves forward. Four scenes deal twelve-tone rows from a serial draw, which plays every pitch of the octave once before any comes again: plainly, passed between two instruments, with rests and leaps, and in pairs. Four scenes over a bass add two draws on alternate steps, add them together, pull one against the other, and add all three. The last two scenes gate notes by draws: a melody that grows as a register counts the bars, and a bell whose rhythm comes from a draw that its own condition redraws.

Guitarify: 23_guitarify.yml ​

A tour of guitar playing in twenty short scenes, each a way a guitar is played, on seven General MIDI guitars and nothing else: a country bass note and strum, picking with thumb and fingers, bluegrass crosspicking, jazz comping, a blues in open G, power chords, a surf tremolo, the folk strum, funk scratching, a singer's rolled chords, metal gallops and a twelve bar shuffle. Every pattern is only a row of chords or low notes, and the Guitarify generator of each scene does the playing, so the demo shows what its Shape and Strokes sections do to the same kind of pattern.

Hello World: 01_hello_world.yml ​

The smallest set there is: a C major scale up in the first scene, down in the second, and up again in the third while a second track holds a C chord for a quarter note, every note a sixteenth. Three seconds long. Open it to see what a pattern, a track and a scene are, play it first when setting up real-time MIDI (docs/MIDI_SETUP.md), and record it first (docs/RECORDING.md).

Pattern Lists: 29_pattern_lists.yml ​

A hard rock riff in E minor made of four patterns of one bar each: the main figure, an answer, a run up and a stop. Every clip of the riff plays a list of those patterns, as Pattern Lists describes, and every section of the song is the same four bars in another order. The first scene plays the four bars once. The verse and the chorus list them two ways, with the bass listing bars of its own and the drums playing three bars of the beat and a fill. The scenes after show Pattern order: alternate, pendulum, and first, then random. The seventh scene sets Step direction, which plays the steps inside each pattern backward while the patterns keep their order.

Scales: 04_scale.yml ​

Every scale llamatron knows, each run up an octave and back down from D, one scene per scale, in the order of the scale picker. Use it to preview a scale before choosing one. The demo shows that a scene can set its own scale, and that a degree past the top of a scale wraps up an octave.
