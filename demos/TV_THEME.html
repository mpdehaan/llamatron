Light-Jazz Sitcom Theme: aunt_clara.yml ​

A twinkling light-jazz sitcom theme of the sixties in the manner of the Bewitched theme (Howard Greenfield and Jack Keller), with a tune of its own. A swinging ride, a walking bass and plucked strings; a vibraphone with a skipping tune on I vi ii V, a celesta sparkling after each phrase; a bridge through four dominant chords; choruses for a muted trumpet and a flute.

Game-Show Theme: come_on_down.yml ​

A bright seventies game-show theme in the manner of The Price Is Right theme (Edd Kalehoff), with a tune of its own. A brass section and a synthesizer in unison on a leaping tune, a clavinet chopping sixteenths, funky bass octaves and a busy drum kit with a tambourine; a bridge, a flute chorus and a drum break.

Slap-Bass Sitcom Theme: eats_cats.yml ​

A bouncing mid-eighties sitcom instrumental in the manner of the ALF theme (Alf Clausen), with a tune of its own. A slap bass alone, then a drum machine and an electric piano; a syncopated synth brass tune over I, vi and the flat seventh; a second strain and a sax chorus.

Sitcom Theme: forgot_your_name.yml ​

A warm piano sitcom theme in the manner of Cheers (Gary Portnoy), with a melody of its own. A piano alone under a quiet verse on I and V, then the band, strings and a choir for a chorus that opens out on I bVII IV V; a bridge, and the piano alone to end.

Hard-Rock Cartoon Theme: meeting_the_eye.yml ​

A one-minute hard-rock cartoon theme of the mid eighties in the manner of the theme of The Transformers (Ford Kinder and Anne Bryant), with a tune of its own. The band plays the slogan's rhythm alone, then the voices shout it; a chorus on i VII i V and VI VII i that a robot voice answers; one low verse; a drum break with the robot alone; the chorus and the slogan again.

Big-Band Police Theme: tropical_51.yml ​

A big-band police-show theme in the manner of Hawaii Five-0 (Morton Stevens), with a fanfare of its own. Tom-toms and timpani open, then a trumpet fanfare in bold leaps over a driving bass, tambourine and brass on the offbeats, the chords falling i VII VI to a major V; trombones answer; and the whole thing rises a step, twice.

Ragtime Sitcom Theme: uk_butler.yml ​

A saloon-piano ragtime sitcom theme in the manner of the Mr. Belvedere theme (Judy Hart-Angelo and Gary Portnoy, as Leon Redbone sang it), with a tune of its own. A honky-tonk piano opens alone with a rag figure over a low note and a chord by turns; a lazy tune over ragtime chords, the piano filling each gap; the piano takes a chorus in octaves; a scatted tag on a muted trumpet.
