The demo songs in the TV theme style. Each is described below, with the features it uses; About Demos lists every demo.

  • Creepy Harpsichord Theme: mysterious_spooky.yml

Sitcom Theme: forgot_your_name.yml ​

A warm sitcom theme in the manner of Cheers, with a melody of its own on clarinet, over a piano comping on the beats, a bass and soft drums.

Spy Theme: spy_hunting.yml ​

A late-fifties spy theme in the manner of Peter Gunn: a low guitar riff doubled by the bass, a surf beat, brass stabs and a tenor saxophone, every scene one chorus of a minor twelve-bar blues from a Progression. The riff and the tune are its own.

Voice-Led Piano Theme: uphill_cops.yml ​

A gentle theme in the manner of Hill Street Blues, with a melody of its own on solo piano. The chords come from a Progression and then a Voice leading generator, which voices each chord nearest the one before, so that the chords glide.
