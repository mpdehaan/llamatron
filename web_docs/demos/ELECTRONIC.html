Articulate: 15_acid_articulate.yml ​

An acid house line played the whole way through, whose notes lengthen from short blips into long, gliding, joined notes over each scene while its filter opens, with no gate changed. The demo sets Articulate on the scenes, on the line itself and as a generator that bounces between short and long every bar, and a register bound to CC 74 opens the filter as the notes lengthen.

examples/learning/15_acid_articulate.yml · A minor · 120 BPM · 3:00 · 9 scenes · 6 tracks

Glide and Shorten: 14_acid_glide.yml ​

Fluid acid techno for a synth with portamento: one sixteen-note acid line from start to finish, and Glide and Shorten rows of 5, 7 and 3 steps putting its slides and short notes somewhere new each bar, though the line itself never changes.

Density: edm_density.yml ​

A club track at 128 beats a minute in F minor whose every intro, build, breakdown and drop comes from Density, which keeps a share of a clip's notes, the strongest places in the bar first. The same seven patterns, each written at the busiest it will ever play, run through every scene, and only the densities change: the scenes' own ramps fill the intro and the builds in and strip the outro away, and four Density generators, sparse, half, rise and fall, thin the breakdown, turn the drums half time and make the arp and the lead trade places across the second drop.

examples/by_genre/electronic/edm_density.yml · F minor · 128 BPM · 3:00 · 8 scenes · 7 tracks

The first line of Ode to Joy, slowed down to long notes, and then chopped up. The scenes named Ratchet use a pattern's own Ratchet, Ratchet offset and Probability rows. The scenes named AutoRatchet leave the pattern alone and do the chopping in a chain instead.

Registers: 22_registers.yml ​

An acid techno riff of five notes, changed bar by bar by the four registers, AX to DX. AX, BX and CX each move a different note of the riff, in cycles of 4, 6 and 5 bars, then all three at once. Then DX fades the riff, kick and hat out together over six repeats of the last scene.

examples/learning/22_registers.yml · A minor · 132 BPM · 1:35 · 5 scenes · 3 tracks

Ties: 08_tie.yml ​

An original downtempo piece in D Mixolydian whose pad draws its chords with the Tie row rather than with gates, so a chord lasts as long as the ties that follow it, and whose ending holds a bass note with ties alone, longer than any gate could make it. The demo also brings in the tracks one at a time, morphs a tune into its answer, grabs an echo and slides a filter, so it's the closest thing here to a finished song.

examples/learning/08_tie.yml · D mixolydian · 84 BPM · 2:59 · 9 scenes · 8 tracks
