# FM

The FM drums are planned drum engines, each made by frequency modulation: one
oscillator moving the pitch of another so fast that the movement is heard as
tone, the way bright, metallic and bell-like hits are made. They are agreed
and not built yet; this page will describe them, with their Oscillators tabs
and how they are made, once they are.

## FM Kick

A kick drum made by frequency modulation.

## FM Snare

A snare drum made by frequency modulation.

## FM Tom

A tom made by frequency modulation.

## FM Metal

Metallic percussion made by frequency modulation.

## FM Bell

A bell made by frequency modulation.
