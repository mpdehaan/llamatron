# Experimental

Music that avoids a key on purpose. A **row**, an ordered set of all twelve
pitches, replaces the scale: it is played forward, backward, upside down and
moved to other starting notes, and no pitch returns before the others have
sounded. Sections differ by which form of the row is used, by who plays, and
by density and loudness, since chords and a chorus are not there to mark them.
Rhythm and tone color carry what harmony carries elsewhere. (practice)
