# Soundtrack

Two families a wizard could make.

## The Adventure Score (orchestra)

- **The triplet fanfare**: brass on fast repeated notes in triplets,
  announcing the home chord, before and inside a broad tune. The triplets
  carry on into the tune itself, so march and melody are one gesture.
  Repeated-note brass figures are the signature. (practice)
- **Fanfare intervals**: tunes built on leaps of a fourth, a fifth and a
  seventh, heroic and open, over chords stacked in fourths as well as plain
  triads. The first big leap upward is the theme's identity. (practice)
- **March and ostinato**: a pulsing low figure of one bar, repeated, on
  strings, low brass and timpani, with chords above that may belong to another
  key, for menace; a steady march rhythm under a villain's theme in mid-to-low
  brass. (practice)
- **A theme for each thing** (a person, a place, an idea), short enough to be
  recognized in three or four notes, returning in new dress: loud in brass,
  soft in a solo horn or flute, slow in strings. (practice)
- **Layout**: tune in trumpets or horns, answered or imitated by other brass;
  strings running fast figures or holding the harmony; woodwinds doubling and
  decorating high; timpani and cymbals at arrivals; harp and bells for wonder.
  A quiet middle section, strings and winds, before the fanfare returns.
  (practice)
- **Wonder**, the other face: a slow, wide, hymn-like tune in a major key over
  simple chords, rising by steps to a held high note, strings and horns, no
  march. (practice)
- Forms: a march (fanfare, tune, softer middle, tune again, coda) rather than
  verse and chorus. Triple and compound meters appear beside four. (practice)
