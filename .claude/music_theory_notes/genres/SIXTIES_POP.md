# Sixties Pop

Short songs with the chorus early, many instruments in small parts, a tune in front always. A drum fill in or straight in on the
voice; a tag or a fade at the end. Chords richer than rock's, with the minor fourth and quick changes. (practice)

- **Big studio**: many players doubling each other into one thick sound, castanets and tambourine, a huge backbeat.
- **Sunshine**: bright major keys, stacked voices, light and bouncing.
- **Baroque**: harpsichord, strings or winds beside the band, lines that move like older music.
- **Folk pop**: acoustic guitars, close voices, gentle drums.
- **Soul pop**: a dance backbeat on every beat, tambourine, a busy melodic bass.
- **Bubblegum**: the simplest chords and the most repeated hook, straight in.
- **Waltz ballad**: three or six beats to the bar, keys first, slowing at the end.
