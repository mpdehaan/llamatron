# Jazz

Not a genre of the wizard yet. (practice, from teaching materials)

- **Modal playing**: a scale given for many bars in place of a run of chords; slow or medium; space is a note; melody returns.
- **Vertical playing**: every chord of a fast progression traced in arpeggios and runs, very dense; substitutions that move
  the key by thirds. Later the two meet: fast chord movement laid over one mode.
- **The angular pianist**: flat ninths and fifths, clusters, whole-tone runs; sparse comping with notes left out; a percussive
  touch, silences and hesitations; a riff repeated and shifted by two beats so its accents fall anew; behind or ahead of the
  beat on purpose over very strong time.
- **Electric vamps**: one or two chords, a bass ostinato, long forms that grow by texture. (thin)
- **The organ trio and groove jazz**: organ, drums and a guitar or a bass; the organist can be bass, chords and lead; funk and
  hip-hop beats with free passages; the process of jazz kept, the style optional; at home with jam bands.
