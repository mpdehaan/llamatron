# Television Themes

Two families: the family comedy's opening song and the adventure series' orchestral theme. Principles only. Much of what can be
read about them is fan writing, so most of this is practice and judgment, and thin: to be deepened before either is built.

## The Family Comedy Theme (1980s)

- **What it is for**: a whole song in under a minute, heard every week, that says the show is warm and safe. Memorable first,
  and nostalgic by design: it should sound like looking at a photo album. (practice)
- **Shape**: no intro to speak of (a pickup, a fill or one bar), a short verse of 8 bars, then the chorus with the title or the
  show's idea in it, and a tag: the last line again, slower or held, with a closing flourish. 45 to 70 seconds; a 20 to 30
  second cut for later seasons is the chorus and the tag alone. Nothing may wait: the hook arrives within 15 seconds. (practice)
- **Sound**: adult contemporary soft rock. Electric piano or a bell-like digital piano carrying the chords, a clean guitar with
  chorus, a round fretless or synth bass, light drums with gated reverb and a side stick in the verse, strings or a soft pad
  behind, and a saxophone, harmonica or lead guitar taking the fills and the last word. (practice)
- **Voices**: one warm singer, or a man and a woman trading lines and joining in harmony on the chorus; stacked harmonies on the
  title. Where the theme is instrumental, a saxophone or a whistled or piano tune takes the singer's place. (practice)
- **Tune**: singable by a family, in a major key, mostly steps, within an octave and a bit, the title on the longest and highest
  notes, each phrase answered by the next. The chorus's last line climbs to a held note. (practice)
- **Chords**: major keys; I, IV, V, vi and ii, with added ninths and major sevenths for warmth, a IV over V before each arrival,
  slash chords with a walking or falling bass, and often a key change up a step or a semitone for the last line. (practice)
- **Tempo and feel**: medium, 90 to 125, straight eighths or a gentle shuffle; a few are slow ballads. (practice)
- **The other kinds of the decade**, for contrast: the funk and jazz instrumental with slap bass and saxophone; the solo piano
  and voice that opens small and swells; the ironic use of an old standard. (practice)

## The Adventure Series Theme

See `SOUNDTRACK.md`, "The Adventure Score": the television theme is that score in 60 to 90 seconds. To research when reached:
the opening fanfare over a spoken or silent teaser, the broad tune in horns over a march or a pulsing ostinato, the softer
second strain, the return and a final held chord; and the later, darker kind built on drums, voices and a slow modal tune.
