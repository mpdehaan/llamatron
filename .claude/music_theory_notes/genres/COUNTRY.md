# Country and Bluegrass

Not a genre of the wizard yet. (practice, from lessons)

- **Boom-chick**: bass note on one and three alternating root and fifth, a strum of two strings on two and four.
- **Train beat**: snare brushed in steady eighths or sixteenths with accents on two and four, like a train; the outlaw kind
  leans on the downbeat and drives.
- **Bluegrass**: no drums; guitar and bass are the foundation, the mandolin chops the backbeat as the snare would, banjo rolls
  in steady notes, fiddle and resonator guitar weave fills and trade solos; high close harmony.
