# Soul and Funk

The groove is the song: drums and bass locked, every other part a small rhythm in the gaps. Horns answer the singer and punch
accents. Few chords, sometimes one. Ghost notes everywhere. (practice)

- **Sixties soul**: a drum fill in, a steady backbeat, horns answering, a tag ending.
- **Southern soul**: slower and heavier, the backbeat laid back, horns in long notes and stabs.
- **Soul ballad**: keys first, twelve-eight or slow four, the band swelling at the chorus.
- **Funk**: drums first, kicks off the beat, sixteenth-note guitar scratches, the first beat of the bar hit hard by all; a hard stop.
- **New orleans**: a rolling, marching snare feel between straight and swung, piano forward.
- **Disco**: kick on every beat, open hi-hat on the off-beats, octave bass, strings; layers in and layers out.
