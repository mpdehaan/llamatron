# Rock

Guitars, bass and drums with a backbeat; the riff and the chorus are the song. Verse and chorus forms of eight-bar sections, a
bridge or a solo late. Major and minor keys with the flat seventh chord borrowed freely. (practice)

- **Rock and roll**: twelve-bar or three-chord songs, fast, a shuffle or a straight eighth feel, a drum fill in, a hard stop.
- **Beat group**: tight songs, sung harmonies, a counted or filled start, clear endings; chords richer than three.
- **Folk rock**: strummed or picked guitar first, light drums, the lead instrument sparing; held endings.
- **Hard rock**: the riff first, on guitar, then drums and bass together; heavy backbeat, big fills, a big finish.
- **Arena rock**: built in layers toward a large chorus; the hook stated before the verse; solos; a big finish.
- **Punk**: fast, counted in or straight in, everyone at once, few fills, no ghost notes, a hard stop, hardly a solo.
- **New wave**: drums and bass or keys first, tight and dry, eighth-note bass, synth hooks; held or riff endings.
- **Rock ballad**: keys or a clean guitar alone first, the band entering at a chorus, slowing at the end.
